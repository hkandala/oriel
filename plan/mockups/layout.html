Oriel main window: sidebar shown and hidden

Static mock-ups for review (dark, Glass). Sample data: one machine (This Mac) with three workspaces. Built by plan/mockups/build.py.

A. Sidebar shown

The sidebar button (left of the title row) is always there; ⌃⌘S does the same. The sidebar shows one machine page: herdr's workspaces as sections, their tabs as rows. A collapsed section (infra) shows how many agents need you. Page dots appear once there is more than one machine. The title-row button shows the machine.

C. Sidebar shown, Activity on (eye)

The eye filters the sidebar: only tabs whose agents work, need you or finished and are not seen yet, each with a second line (what happened, how long ago). The tab you are on stays listed while selected. A seen tab leaves the list when you move to another tab.
