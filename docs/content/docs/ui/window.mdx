## Title row

One row, 40 pt tall (D45). From left to right:
- The sidebar toggle.
- The session button: a laptop icon, plus the session name (up to 224 pt)
  only when more than one herdr session runs. A click opens the session list.
- The tab bar, while the sidebar is hidden.
- On the right: the eye (Activity, ⌘.), ⌘ (the command palette) and `+` (new
  tab).

The eye is `eye`, and `eye.fill` while Activity is on. It has a dot badge:
amber when an agent needs you, else green when a finish is not seen yet.

Each title-row button and each card header button shows a tooltip after the
pointer rests on it for 0.5 s: a small glass label with the action's name and
its shortcut as keycap chips, for example "New Tab ⌘T". It fades in over
0.13 s.

The window title is "‹tab title› - ‹session›", so the Window menu and Mission
Control show it.

## Tab bar

The tab bar is in the title row while the sidebar is hidden. While the sidebar
is shown, the tabs are its rows under each workspace (D45). One capsule per
herdr tab (D42).
- **Track.** A capsule 34 pt tall sits behind the tabs. It is hidden when the
  workspace has one tab.
- **Width.** All tabs share one width: the space less the 1 pt gaps, divided
  by the count, at most 220 pt and at least 64 pt. Below 64 pt the bar scrolls
  sideways, with edge fades (8 pt leading, 16 pt trailing), and keeps the
  selected tab in view. The setting **Tabs fill the bar** removes the 220 pt
  limit.
- **After a close**, the widths stay as they were while the pointer stays in
  the bar, so the next × is under the pointer. They settle when the pointer
  leaves.
- **Dividers.** 1 pt wide, the tab height less 18 pt tall, centered between
  tabs. A divider next to the selected, a lit or the hovered tab is hidden. They
  fade over 0.2 s.
- **Selection.** One marker follows the strip as it scrolls, so it sits
  between two capsules while the strip is between their pages. Its radius is
  half its height, at most 16.5 pt. A click, ⌘1…⌘9 or a key step moves it with
  Rex's spring (stiffness 520, damping 46). Reduce Motion makes it jump. Glass
  draws it as a Liquid Glass capsule, Classic as a plain raised pill.
- **Hover.** The hovered tab gets the hover fill over 0.14 s.
- **Content.** The tab's program icon stack (up to 5 icons, the keyboard
  card's in front, [Program icons](/docs/ui/program-icons)), the title, and
  one status slot.
- **Title.** One line. When it does not fit, its end fades out over 16 pt; it
  never shows an ellipsis. The folder part is in secondary text. The title is
  herdr's tab label. When the label is herdr's default (a bare number), the
  title is the keyboard card's program and folder, for example "zsh ~" (D65).
- **Status slot.** × on the selected tab and on the hovered one (a 22 pt hit
  area, hidden when the tab is narrower than 52 pt). Otherwise three working
  dots, on every tab with a working agent except the selected one. Needs you
  and finished are badges on the front icon
  ([Theme and accessibility](/docs/ui/theme-and-accessibility#status)).
- **Announcement.** When a tab that is not selected turns to needs you or
  finished, its front icon bounces once and its title shows "‹agent› needs
  you" or "‹agent› finished" for 3 s. A long text scrolls once (30 pt/s)
  instead of fading. Reduce Motion turns the bounce off.
- **Context menu** on a capsule and on a sidebar tab row: Rename Tab, Move Tab
  Left, Move Tab Right, Close Tab, Close Other Tabs. Close Other Tabs asks once,
  with the total pane count. Rename puts a text field in place of the title:
  Enter saves, Esc cancels.
- **Keycap hints.** If the user holds ⌘ for 0.7 s while the window is key,
  each of the first nine tabs (capsules, or the rows of the selected workspace)
  shows a keycap "⌘1"… over its icon. They fade in over 0.13 s and out over
  0.1 s when ⌘ is released.
- Drag a capsule to reorder the tabs ([Drag and drop](/docs/ui/drag-and-drop)).
- Rest the pointer on a capsule to see the [hover card](#hover-card).

## Sidebar

Flat on the window background, with no panel (D44).
- Middle: the machine pages, swiped sideways. Each page starts with the
  machine's name in a small line.
- Below them, fixed: page dots (one per machine, clickable).
- Bottom, fixed: a Filter field, New Workspace and Add Machine.
- While Activity is on, the sidebar lists [Activity](#activity) instead of
  the machine page.
- **Width.** 240 pt by default. Drag the 6 pt handle at the trailing edge to
  resize it between 227 and 480 pt. The width is kept per device. During the
  drag the strip keeps its pages whole.

A machine is one herdr session ([machine](/docs/reference/glossary)). The
machine page of a non-default session on this Mac is named
"This Mac · ‹session›".

**Now.** One machine page, the Filter field, New Workspace and Add Remote Host
(a placeholder). Machine pages and agent rows come later.

## Machine page

- herdr's workspaces, in herdr's order, as section headers, with their tabs
  as rows.
- **Rows.** 30 pt tall, 2 pt apart: the icon stack (up to 3 icons), the title
  and the status slot. The selected tab is a raised pill, radius 10 (D45).
- **Sticky headers.** A workspace header stays at the top while its tabs
  scroll under it. 18 pt fades show at the top and bottom of the list when
  content is under them.
- **Collapse.** A chevron collapses a section (it turns over 0.18 s). A
  collapsed workspace shows the number of its agents that need you, in the
  needs-you color, and the working dots when one works. The collapse state is
  kept per device.
- **Header actions.** On hover, a `+` at the trailing end makes a new tab in
  that workspace, in the folder of its keyboard card (or `$HOME`). The header's
  context menu: New Tab, Rename Workspace, Move Up, Move Down, Close Workspace.
  Close Workspace always asks, with the tab and pane count.
- **Rename.** A double-click on a workspace or tab title puts a text field in
  its place: Enter saves, Esc cancels.
- Drag rows and headers to reorder them
  ([Drag and drop](/docs/ui/drag-and-drop)).
- Later a workspace also lists its agent panes (icon, name, status), and the
  collapsed count covers them.
- Clicking a workspace selects it in the app only
  ([Selection and "seen" are the app's own](/docs/layout#selection-and-seen-are-the-apps-own)).

## Activity

The list of agents that need you, finished or work (D61). The eye, ⌘. or
View ▸ Show Activity turns it on and off.
- With the sidebar shown, the sidebar lists Activity instead of the tabs. With
  the sidebar hidden, the same list is a floating glass panel under the eye,
  320 pt wide and at most 480 pt tall. Esc closes the panel.
- One section per workspace. One row per agent pane that needs you or has a
  finish not seen yet, and the working ones while **Show working agents** (the
  list's footer toggle) is on.
- **Row.** 44 pt: the program tile, the title (the agent's name, else the tab
  title) and a status capsule on the right: "Needs you" (amber), "Done"
  (green) or "Working" (blue), text in the color on a 0.15 fill of it. The
  second line has the tab label in secondary text and the age on the right
  ("now", "3m", "2h"), counted from the time the app saw the change.
- **Order.** Needs you, then done, then working; newest first inside each.
  Rows do not move while the pointer is over the list.
- A click selects the workspace and tab and gives that pane the keyboard, so
  the finish is seen.
- With nothing to show: "Nothing needs you".

How it works: [Activity and notifications](/docs/status#activity-and-notifications).

## Hover card

A peek at what runs in a tab (D63).
- **When.** The pointer rests 0.25 s on a title-bar capsule, a sidebar tab row
  or an Activity row. The card stays while the pointer moves into it (0.15 s
  grace). It goes when the pointer leaves, on a click elsewhere, on a scroll,
  and with Esc. One card at a time, and none during a drag.
- **Where.** Centered 6 pt under a capsule, or 6 pt right of the sidebar edge
  for a row. It stays inside the window.
- **Look.** A glass panel, radius 12, at most 280 pt wide. It fades and grows
  in from 0.97 over 0.13 s.
- **Content**, top to bottom:
  - The tab title (folder part dimmed) and the tab's status capsule, if any.
  - A minimap of the tab's splits, 56 pt tall: each card a small rounded
    rect (radius 2, 2 pt gap), the keyboard card in the accent color. Hidden
    for a tab with one pane.
  - One row per pane, at most 6 and then "+N more": the program glyph, the
    program or agent name, the folder in secondary text, and the status
    capsule or the working dots on the right.
  - For a pane that needs you: the last 3 non-empty lines of its screen, in
    secondary monospaced text. The app reads them with `pane.read` when the
    card opens, one call per such pane, and shows nothing if the read fails.
- A click on a pane row selects the workspace and tab and gives that pane the
  keyboard. A click on the title selects the tab.
- VoiceOver: the card is a popover with the rows as buttons. A tab's action
  "Show Panes" opens it.

## Strip

- One page per tab, full width, paged.
- Inside a page, pane cards follow herdr's split tree, with an 8 pt gap
  between them. The gap is the divider: drag it to resize (a grip shows on
  hover), double-click it to reset it to 0.5, and ⌃⌘= balances the tab.
- Cards without the keyboard fade (D64). Layout changes animate.

How it works: [Layout and sizing](/docs/layout).

## Pane card

- **Card.** Radius 10. It fades in over 0.2 s when its terminal's first frame
  arrives.
- **Header.** 36 pt tall: the program glyph, the title (herdr's pane label if
  set, else the program) and the folder in secondary text. On the trailing
  side, four 22 pt buttons, 2 pt apart: Split Right, Split Down, Zoom and
  Close. Zoom is disabled when the tab has one pane.
- On macOS the buttons show on the keyboard card and on a hovered card. On iOS
  they always show.
- Agent and terminal cards also have a chat|term view switch, plugin panes a
  plugin|term switch. Plugin and web cards also have **Refresh**, which loads
  the card again from the machine
  ([When a card reads its state](/docs/plugins/state#when-a-card-reads-its-state)).
- **Show pane headers** (a setting, on by default). When it is off, the card
  is all content, and its actions stay in the context menu, the menu bar and
  the palette.
- **Context menu** on the header and on the card's edge (inside the terminal,
  the program keeps its own right-click): Split Right, Split Down, Split Left,
  Split Up, Zoom or Restore, Move to New Tab, Move to Tab ▸ (the workspace's
  other tabs), Move to New Workspace, Rename Pane…, Close Pane.
- **Rename Pane** puts a text field in the header. Enter saves the label; an
  empty name clears it. Labels that start with `oriel:` belong to the app and
  are not offered for rename.
- Drag the header to move the pane ([Drag and drop](/docs/ui/drag-and-drop)).
- Close asks first unless the pane runs only its shell.
- ⌘-click on a link in a terminal opens it in the default browser, `http` and
  `https` only.

## Find bar

⌘F opens the find bar on the keyboard card.
- A small glass bar at the card's top right, 8 pt in: the field, the match
  count ("3 of 12"), ↑ and ↓ buttons, and ×. One bar per card.
- ⌘G or Enter goes to the next match, ⇧Enter to the previous one. Esc closes
  the bar.
- The text comes from herdr and the search is case blind. A match scrolls
  herdr's viewport into view and gets a highlight. Closing the bar scrolls back
  to the bottom. herdr's viewport is shared, so a herdr TUI on the same pane
  scrolls too.

How it works: [Scrolling, queries and links](/docs/talking-to-herdr#scrolling-queries-and-links).

## Zoom

- The zoom button, a double-click on the header, or ⇧⌘↩ zooms a card. It
  fills its tab's page in the strip, as if it were the only pane. Only the
  zoomed card resizes; the others stay, hidden, at their size.
- A zoomed card shows only two buttons: Restore (white on an accent circle)
  and Close.
- A split from the zoomed card restores the layout first, so the new pane
  shows.
- Restore or ⇧⌘↩ restores the layout. Esc is not used, because the program in
  the pane needs it.

## Notice

A short message after a failed action or a refused move.
- A capsule at the bottom center of the strip, 30 pt above its bottom: glass,
  a leading symbol in a tone color (red for an error, amber for a warning,
  blue for information), the text, an optional action button (for example
  "Retry") and ×.
- It goes after 4 s.

## Command palette

⌘K, ⇧⌘P or the title-bar ⌘ button opens it (D48).
- 480 pt wide. The search field says "Search commands, tabs and sessions…".
- **Rows.** 28 pt: a symbol, the title, and on the right the shortcut in
  secondary glyphs, or › for a row that opens a sub-page. The selected row is
  an accent pill, radius 8.
- **Sections.** With an empty field: the five commands run last (kept in
  memory), then the page's items. Then Commands, Tabs, Workspaces and Sessions.
- **Footer.** Under a hairline, keycap chips: "↩ Run", "⎋ Close", and "⌫ Back"
  on a sub-page.
- **Matching.** Fuzzy, in the order of the typed letters, over titles and
  keywords: "split" finds Split Right, "dark" finds Appearance ▸ Dark. On a
  tie, commands come before tabs, and shorter titles first.
- **Sub-pages.** ⌫ on an empty field or Esc goes back one page; Esc on the
  first page closes the palette.
  - Change Session ›: the sessions, running ones first.
  - Rename Tab ›, Rename Workspace ›, Rename Pane ›: the field holds the
    current name; Enter saves.
  - Move Pane to Tab ›: the workspace's tabs, New Tab and New Workspace.
  - Go to Directory › (⇧⌘G): a folder browser that starts in the keyboard
    card's folder. → or Enter goes into a folder, ← goes up, ⌘↩ chooses the
    shown folder. Choosing types `cd -- '‹path›'` and Return into the card.
    Enabled only when the card runs only its shell.
  - Insert Path › (⌃⌘I): the same browser with files too. Choosing types the
    shell-quoted path at the cursor, without Return.
  - Appearance › and Interface Style ›: a row highlighted for 0.75 s previews
    on the window. Enter keeps it; Esc restores the old one.
  - Font Size ›, Cursor Style ›.
- **Settings as commands.** Each on/off setting is one command whose title
  says what it does now: "Turn On Focus Follows Mouse" or "Turn Off Focus
  Follows Mouse".

The palette and the menu bar come from one command list, so each title and
shortcut is written once
([Keyboard shortcuts](/docs/ui/theme-and-accessibility#keyboard-shortcuts)).

How it works: [Layout and sizing](/docs/layout),
[Zoom is the app's own](/docs/layout#zoom-is-the-apps-own),
[Platform: tab bar and Liquid Glass](/docs/platform#tab-bar-and-liquid-glass).
