## What herdr allows

Oriel offers only moves that herdr can make, and builds the rest from herdr
calls (D69). What herdr 0.9.5 allows, tested on a throwaway session:

| herdr call | Allowed | Refused |
|---|---|---|
| `pane.swap {source_pane_id, target_pane_id}` (or `{pane_id, direction}`) | two panes of the same tab | panes of different tabs (`changed:false`, `reason:"cross_tab"`) |
| `pane.move {destination:{type:"tab", tab_id, split, target_pane_id?, ratio?}}` | into another tab of any workspace of the session, split right or down beside `target_pane_id` (or, when it is left out, the tab's focused pane), with an optional ratio | into its own tab (`reason:"same_tab"`) |
| `pane.move {destination:{type:"new_tab", workspace_id?, label?}}` | a new tab in any workspace of the session | — |
| `pane.move {destination:{type:"new_workspace", label?, tab_label?}}` | a new workspace | — |
| `tab.move {tab_id, insert_index}` | reorder inside its workspace | a move to another workspace |
| `workspace.move {workspace_id, insert_index}`, `workspace.move_block` | reorder workspaces | — |

- A pane moved to another workspace gets a new pane id. Its terminal id
  stays, so its card stays.
- Moving the last pane of a tab closes that tab.
- Nothing moves between sessions or machines: each session is its own herdr
  server.
- herdr splits only right or down. A drop on a left or top edge is a move (or
  a split) and then a swap.

## Panes

Drag a card by its header (the title area, not its buttons) by 4 pt or more.

![Dragging a pane onto the right edge of another card](/mockups/K.jpg)

- **Preview.** The drag image is a lifted card of one fixed shape, 240 × 150
  pt: the card's top (header and terminal) scaled to cover it, its 10 pt
  corners rounded, at 0.85 with a soft shadow, the pointer over the header
  where it grabbed it. Where the
  terminal's Metal layer does not draw into the snapshot, the card's fill
  shows. The card in place dims to 0.4.
- **Indicator.** A rounded rect (radius 10) filled with the accent color at
  0.2, with a 2 pt accent stroke. It moves between targets over 0.12 s.

| Drop on | Indicator | Result | herdr calls |
|---|---|---|---|
| the center of another card in the same tab (all but the edge bands) | the whole card | swap the two panes | `pane.swap` |
| an edge band of another card in the same tab: the outer quarter on each side is that edge | that half of the card | put the pane beside that card | `pane.move` to a new tab, then `pane.move` back beside the target (right or down), then `pane.swap` for left or top |
| the pane's own tab capsule | none | not a target (herdr refuses `same_tab`) | — |
| another tab's capsule in the title row | the capsule highlighted | move into that tab, split right of its last card | `pane.move {type:"tab", tab_id, split:"right", target_pane_id: the last card}` |
| an edge band of a card in another tab (after spring-loading) | that half of the card | split beside that card | `pane.move {type:"tab", tab_id, split, target_pane_id}`, then `pane.swap` for left or top |
| the empty end of the tab track, or `+` | a ghost capsule at the end (and the `+` highlighted) | a new tab in the selected workspace | `pane.move {type:"new_tab", workspace_id}` |
| a workspace row in the sidebar | the row highlighted | a new tab in that workspace | `pane.move {type:"new_tab", workspace_id}` |
| empty space at the bottom of the sidebar page, or the page header's `+` (a full page has no empty space) | a ghost row "New Workspace", or the `+` highlighted | a new workspace | `pane.move {type:"new_workspace"}` |
| another session's page dots, the Agents view | none; not a target | — | — |

- **Spring-loading.** Hold over a tab capsule for 0.65 s: the strip pages to
  that tab and the capsule pulses once (to 1.04 over 0.2 s). Then drop on a
  card there. Hold over a workspace row for 0.65 s: the workspace is
  selected, so its tabs show and the drop can go on a capsule or a card.
  The pane's own tab's capsule does not spring-load.
- **Equal splits.** A pane moved into a tab (an edge or a capsule) then gets
  an equal share of the run of same-direction splits that holds it, as after
  a split.
- **A step fails.** If a later step of a drop fails, the pane stays where it
  got to, and the notice says so ("Moved the pane to a new tab: herdr refused
  the next step").
- Every `pane.move` passes `focus:false`. Each step uses the pane id from
  herdr's reply to the step before; the app reads the snapshot after the
  drop. If herdr refuses or changes nothing, the notice says why ("The tab
  is zoomed in herdr" when a herdr TUI zoomed the tab, else herdr's reason).
- After the drop, the page shows the pane's tab and the pane has the
  keyboard.
- Esc cancels a drag. A failed write leaves the layout as herdr has it.
- The card's context menu has the same moves (Move to New Tab, Move to Tab ▸,
  Move to New Workspace). That is the only path on iPhone.

## Tabs and workspaces

| Drag | Result | herdr call |
|---|---|---|
| a tab capsule along the tab track | reorder the workspace's tabs; the others slide apart over 0.22 s | `tab.move` |
| a tab capsule onto a workspace row | not a target: herdr moves tabs only inside a workspace | — |
| a workspace row up or down its page | reorder the session's workspaces; the rows slide apart over 0.22 s; a repository's linked worktrees move with it; a worktree moves only among its repository's worktrees | `workspace.move_block {workspace_ids, before_workspace_id}`, as Move Up and Move Down |

- A dragged capsule or row dims to 0.4 in place while the system drag image
  follows the pointer.
- While the sidebar's filter hides rows, workspace rows do not reorder.

How it works: [Writes are herdr calls](/docs/talking-to-herdr#writes-are-herdr-calls).
