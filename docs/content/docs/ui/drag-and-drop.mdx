## Panes

Drag a card by its header (the title area, not its buttons) by 4 pt or more.
With pane headers hidden, hold ⌥ and drag the card's edge.

<DropZones caption="Center: a card in the same tab only. Edges: a card in another tab." />

- **Preview.** A snapshot of the card (its header and the top of its
  content) at half size follows the pointer. The card in place dims to 0.4.
- **Indicator.** A rounded rect (radius 10) filled with the accent color at
  0.2, with a 2 pt accent stroke. It moves between targets over 0.12 s.

| Drop on | Indicator | Result |
|---|---|---|
| the center third of a card in the same tab | the whole card | swap the two panes |
| an edge band (the outer quarter) of a card in another tab | that half of the card | split there |
| a tab capsule or a sidebar tab row | the capsule or row lit | move into that tab, split right |
| the empty end of the tab track, or `+` | a ghost capsule at the end | new tab in this workspace |
| a workspace header in the sidebar | the header lit | new tab in that workspace |
| empty sidebar space | a ghost row "New Workspace" | new workspace |

- **Spring-loading.** Hold over a capsule or row for 0.65 s: that tab is
  selected, the strip pages there and the capsule pulses once (to 1.04 over
  0.2 s). Then drop on a card there.
- Inside one tab only swap is offered. A pane cannot move to another machine.
- One write per drop, then the snapshot. If herdr refuses or changes nothing,
  its reason shows in the notice.
- Esc cancels a drag. A failed write leaves the layout as herdr has it.
- The card's context menu has the same moves (Move to New Tab, Move to Tab ▸,
  Move to New Workspace). That is the only path on iPhone.

## Tabs and workspaces

- Drag a title-bar capsule or a sidebar tab row to reorder the tabs of its
  workspace. The others slide apart over 0.22 s. A tab cannot move to another
  workspace, because herdr moves tabs only inside one.
- Drag a sidebar section header to reorder the workspaces.

How it works: [Writes are herdr calls](/docs/talking-to-herdr#writes-are-herdr-calls).
