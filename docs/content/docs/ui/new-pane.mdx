Approved, being built in phase 35 (the browser in phase 36). A new pane asks
what it runs: **Terminal**, **Agent** or **Browser**. Terminal is selected, so
⌘T and then Return gives a terminal with no click.

## Which panes show it

- Every pane the app creates with a new shell: ⌘T and the title row's `+`,
  ⌘D, ⇧⌘D, Split Left and Split Up, ⌃⌘T and every New Workspace (the page
  header's `+`, the workspace menu, a drop on New Workspace, a folder dropped
  on the Dock icon), New Tab in a workspace's menu, and the first workspace of
  a session the app started.
- The herdr pane and its shell start at once behind the chooser, as without
  it. Nothing in herdr waits for the pick.
- Panes made outside the app (herdr's TUI, its CLI, another device) and panes
  that already run something show their terminal as before. Moved panes
  (Move Pane to Tab, a drag) keep what they show.
- **Replace Pane** (⌘R) on a terminal gives a fresh shell with no chooser. On
  an agent or browser pane it gives a fresh shell with the chooser.
- The chooser is the app's own state, kept on the pane in the app, so every
  window of the app shows it on that pane. herdr, its TUI and other devices
  see a plain shell. After a relaunch, a pane still at the chooser shows its
  terminal.
- The chooser leaves without a pick when the pane starts to run something else
  (a program other than the shell, or an agent that herdr reports), for
  example when someone types into it from herdr's TUI. Then the terminal
  shows.

## Look

A glass panel in the middle of the card, over the waiting terminal. The
terminal stays hidden under it (the card shows only its fill), so no prompt
text shows through the glass.

- **Panel.** Floating glass, as the palette has it
  ([Theme](/docs/ui/theme-and-accessibility#appearance-and-interface-style)):
  radius 18, 24 pt of padding on every side, 408 pt wide and 196 pt tall.
  It is centered on the card's area under the header. It looks the same in
  Glass and Classic, and follows Light and Dark by itself.
- **Header row**, 20 pt tall: "New Pane" in 13 pt semibold, secondary. On a
  remote machine "New Pane on ‹machine›". 16 pt under it, the options.
- **Options.** Three tiles side by side, 112 × 112 pt each, 12 pt apart,
  radius 14:
  - at the top, 22 pt in, the kind's symbol at 28 pt regular: `terminal`,
    `sparkles`, `globe`;
  - 10 pt under it, the name in 13 pt medium: "Terminal", "Agent", "Browser";
  - under the name, one line in 11 pt secondary: "Shell", "Coding agent",
    "Web page". Until the browser is built, Browser's line says "Coming
    soon";
  - in the top right corner, 8 pt in, its number key ("1", "2", "3") in 10 pt
    tertiary.
- **Selection.** A tile is the primary color at 0.04. The selected tile is
  the accent at 0.18 with a 1.5 pt accent edge at 0.6, and its symbol in the
  accent. It moves in the same frame, with no fade, as in a menu.
- **Increase Contrast** gives every tile a 1 pt edge in the primary color at
  0.25, and the secondary and tertiary text the system's stronger colors.
- **Motion.** The panel comes with the card (a new card slides in with it).
  A step change cross-fades over 0.15 s. After a pick the panel fades out over
  0.15 s. Reduce Motion makes each change at once. Reduce Transparency is
  handled by the system glass.

### Small cards and floating windows

- When the card's area under the header is narrower than 440 pt or lower
  than 228 pt (the panel and 16 pt on each side), the panel turns into a
  list: as wide as the area less 16 pt, at most 280 pt, 12 pt of padding, the
  same header row, and one 32 pt row per option (the symbol at 16 pt, the
  name in 13 pt, the number key at the row's end), 4 pt apart. The selected
  row is the palette's accent pill, radius 8.
- A card smaller than the list cuts it like any content. The keys still work.
- In a [floating window](/docs/ui/window#floating-pane) (Replace Pane on a
  floated agent pane) the chooser sits in the middle of the window's card,
  with the same rules.
- A zoomed card shows it in the middle of the zoomed card.

## Keys and pointer

The chooser has the keyboard as soon as it shows. The card it sits on is the
keyboard card (a new pane gets the keyboard, as today).
- ← → and ↑ ↓ move the selection. They stop at the ends.
- Return picks the selected option at once, on both steps.
- 1, 2 and 3 pick that option at once (1 to 4 on the Agent step).
- Esc on the first step picks Terminal. Esc on the Agent step goes back to the
  first step, with Agent selected.
- The pointer moves the selection, as in the palette. A click picks.
- Other keys do nothing. The app's shortcuts (⌘T, ⌘W, ⌘K, ⌘[ and others) work
  as usual. When the keyboard moves to another card, the chooser stays on its
  card and has the keyboard again when the card does.

## Terminal

The panel fades out and the terminal shows with its usual fade-in
([Pane card](/docs/ui/window#pane-card)), with the keyboard. The shell has
been running since the pane was made, so there is no wait.

## Agent

The same panel shows its second step. The header row has a back button (a
22 pt `chevron.left`, as the card header's buttons) and "Agent". Under it,
four tiles, 84 × 112 pt, 8 pt apart:

| Tile | Name | Command |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Claude Code | `claude` |
| 2 | Codex | `codex` |
| 3 | pi | `pi` |
| 4 | opencode | `opencode` |

- Each tile shows the agent's program tile at twice its size (44 × 36 pt,
  [Program icons](/docs/ui/program-icons)), the name in 12 pt medium, and the
  command in 11 pt secondary monospaced text. Claude Code is selected first.
- All four always show. The app does not look for which agents are installed.
- A pick types the command and Return into the pane's shell, as Go to
  Directory types its `cd` ([Command palette](/docs/ui/window#command-palette)),
  with no flags. On a remote machine the shell and the agent run on that
  machine.
- **The loader.** The panel fades out and the card shows, in its middle, the
  agent's program tile at 44 × 36 pt, under it a small spinner, and under that
  "Starting ‹agent›…" in 13 pt secondary. The terminal stays hidden.
- **Ready** is the first of these signals:
  - herdr reports that agent on the pane (`agent` set, with a status).
  - the agent's first full screen: herdr's frames stop for 0.3 s after the
    screen left the shell's prompt.

  Phase 35 spikes which signal each agent gives, on This Mac and on a remote
  machine, and writes the result here.
- At ready, the loader fades out over 0.15 s and the terminal fades in with
  its usual fade-in, with the keyboard.
- **Failure.** When the command ends (the shell is the foreground process
  again, asked as after a Return,
  [Program icons](/docs/ui/program-icons#when-the-app-asks-herdr)), or after
  10 s with no ready signal, the terminal shows the same way, with what the
  command printed (for example "command not found: opencode"). No other
  message.

## Browser

Until phase 36, Browser is a placeholder: its tile says "Coming soon", and a
pick shows the terminal as Terminal does.

From phase 36, a pick turns the pane into a browser pane with Replace Pane's
steps: the browser's labeled pane takes the old pane's place and size, and the
old pane closes ([Create](/docs/future-work/web-page#create)). Meanwhile the
card shows the loader with the `globe` symbol and "Opening Browser…". Then the
browser card fades in with an empty address field that has the keyboard
([Browser](/docs/future-work/web-page)).

## Commands that change a pane's kind

Three commands turn the keyboard card into another kind. They are in the File
menu after Replace Pane, in the card's context menu after Replace Pane, and
in the palette. They have no shortcut.

| Command | Shows on | Does |
|---|---|---|
| Open Terminal Here | agent and browser panes | Replace Pane: a fresh shell, with no chooser |
| Start Agent Here ▸ | every pane | a sub-page (in menus a submenu) with Claude Code, Codex, pi and opencode; a pick replaces the pane with a fresh shell and starts that agent in it, with the loader |
| Open Browser Here | every pane but a browser pane (from phase 36) | replaces the pane with a browser pane, with the loader |

- A pane's kind: a browser pane has a browser label, an agent pane is one
  where herdr reports an agent, and every other pane is a terminal (also one
  that runs vim or a server).
- All three use Replace Pane's machinery: the new pane gets the old one's
  place, size and folder, and the keyboard, and a zoomed or floated pane
  stays so ([Pane card](/docs/ui/window#pane-card)).
- They ask first as Replace Pane does, unless the pane runs only its shell or
  is a browser pane (it runs only its placeholder). The dialog is Replace
  Pane's ("Replace “‹name›”?"), and its red button names the command: "Open
  Terminal", "Start ‹agent›" or "Open Browser".
- On a pane that shows the chooser, Start Agent Here and Open Browser Here act
  as the chooser's picks, with no replace.
- In a floating window the palette and the menu bar offer them too.
- The Keyboard Shortcuts panel does not list them (it lists commands with a
  shortcut).

## VoiceOver and identifiers

- The panel is a group named "New Pane". Each tile is a button named by its
  option ("Terminal", "Agent", "Browser", "Claude Code", …), with the
  selected trait on the selected one. Browser's value is "Coming soon" until
  phase 36. The back button is "Back".
- When the chooser shows, VoiceOver moves to the selected tile. The loader
  reads "Starting ‹agent›".
- Identifiers sit on the leaf elements, after the card's `pane.‹id›`:
  `pane.‹id›.chooser.terminal`, `.agent`, `.browser`, `.claude`, `.codex`,
  `.pi`, `.opencode`, `.back`, and `pane.‹id›.loader` for the loader.

## Later

- Plugin pane kinds in the chooser
  ([Pane kinds and plugins](/docs/future-work/plugins#choosing-a-kind)).
