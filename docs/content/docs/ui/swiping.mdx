---
title: "Swiping"
description: "Session pages in the sidebar, and ⌥ + swipe"
---

- Two-finger swipe on a trackpad. Pages snap.
- **Sidebar.** A sideways swipe on the sidebar moves to the next or previous
  session page open in the window ([Sidebar](/docs/ui/window#sidebar)). The
  page dots show where you are, and a click on a dot switches. At the first
  or last page the swipe does nothing. In the Agents view the same swipe
  shows the next session's agents.
- **Everywhere else.** A sideways swipe goes to the content under the
  pointer, as any scroll does: a card's own sideways scroll (over a browser
  card, WebKit's back and forward), the tab bar when its tabs do not fit, or
  nothing. It never changes the tab; tabs
  change with a click, ⇧⌘[ and ⇧⌘], ⌃⇥, ⌘1–9 and the palette, at once
  ([Tab bar](/docs/ui/window#tab-bar)).
- **⌥ + swipe.** With ⌥ (Option) held, a two-finger swipe anywhere in the
  window, over the cards too, moves between sessions sideways and between
  the sidebar's rows up or down. Sideways it moves the sidebar's pages as a
  swipe over them does; with the sidebar hidden, or in the overview, it
  steps one session, as ⌥⌘[ ] do. Up or down it steps one row: the
  workspace above or below, as ⌃⌘[ ] do, or in the Agents view the agent row
  above or below the lit one, going to its pane as a click on the row does (a
  folded group's rows and floated panes are skipped). Fingers up go to the
  row above, down to the one below, whatever the scroll direction. One step a
  gesture; a mouse wheel's burst of clicks with ⌥ steps once too. A swipe
  begun with ⌥ held never scrolls what is under the pointer, its momentum
  neither; ⌥ pressed mid-swipe changes nothing. In a floating pane's window,
  a sheet or under a panel a ⌥ + swipe steps nothing. ⌃ + scroll is the
  system's zoom and ⇧ + scroll scrolls sideways, so neither is used.
- **One target.** The app's scroll monitor locks a gesture to one axis (D35).
  It gives a sideways gesture over the sidebar (its pages, the dots and the
  filter) to the sidebar's pages; any other gesture goes to the view under
  the pointer. Under a panel (the palette, Open Session) the sidebar's pages
  do not move.
- The sidebar's pages follow the fingers one to one. As the fingers lift
  they slide on to the page they land on, starting at the fingers' speed:
  the next page that way, or the nearest page when they move slowly (under
  100 pt a second) or stop before they lift. They slide with a spring of
  response 0.2 s and no bounce (0.14 s felt too quick). At the first and last
  page the pages stop. The app's scroll monitor gives the pages the
  gesture's moves and its lift and drops the momentum. The pages are the
  app's own, not a scroll view's: a scroll view's paging snap is too slow
  (about 0.4 s) and sends a quick flick back to its page. At rest only the
  page in view is laid out; the others come in while the pages move. A swipe
  during a slide takes the pages from where they are. The window shows the
  session once the pages are drawn on their place, within half a point:
  its first draw holds the slide still, so shown at the lift it held the
  slide's start, and shown earlier the pages stopped short and jumped. A
  wheel's sideways burst over the sidebar turns one page. Under Reduce
  Motion the pages switch without motion.
- First visit to a session page: the app shows herdr's focused workspace and
  its active tab. Later visits: the workspace and tab the user last picked
  there, in that window.
- Every swipe also has a keyboard path, a menu path and a button. The keys are
  in [Keyboard shortcuts](/docs/ui/theme-and-accessibility#keyboard-shortcuts).
