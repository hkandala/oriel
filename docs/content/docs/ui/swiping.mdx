---
title: "Swiping"
description: "Session pages in the sidebar and tab pages in the strip"
---

![The sidebar after a swipe to the next open session](/mockups/B.jpg)

- Two-finger swipe on a trackpad, one finger on iOS. Pages snap.
- **Sidebar.** A sideways swipe on the sidebar moves to the next or previous
  session page open in the window ([Sidebar](/docs/ui/window#sidebar)). The
  page dots show where you are, and a click on a dot switches. At the first
  or last page the swipe does nothing. In the Agents view the same swipe
  shows the next session's agents.
- **Strip.** A sideways swipe on the strip pages between the selected
  workspace's tabs (also ⇧⌘[ and ⇧⌘], ⌃⇥).
- **One target.** The app's scroll monitor locks a gesture to one axis (D35).
  It gives a sideways gesture to the innermost scroll view under the pointer
  that can scroll sideways (its content is wider than its frame). Over the
  sidebar, that is the sidebar's pages, not the page's list; over the strip,
  the strip. So swiping the sidebar never pages the strip, and the reverse.
- A tab page slides with the strip's spring. A session page follows the
  fingers, and as they lift it slides on to the page it lands on with a
  spring of response 0.2 s and no bounce (between the tab spring's 0.28 s and
  phase 25's 0.14 s, which felt too quick), starting at the fingers' speed:
  the next page that way, or the nearest page when they move slowly or
  stop before they lift. The app's scroll
  monitor takes the gesture's end and its momentum from the scroll view, so
  the system's own snap (about 0.4 s) never starts. The window shows that
  session as the slide ends: shown at the lift, its first draw held the
  slide still for up to 0.35 s, then it jumped (phase 27). Under
  Reduce Motion they switch without motion. The keys, a click on a tab and the palette show the tab's
  page at once, with no slide.
- First visit to a session page: the app shows herdr's focused workspace and
  its active tab. Later visits: the workspace and tab the user last picked
  there, in that window.
- Every swipe also has a keyboard path, a menu path and a button. The keys are
  in [Keyboard shortcuts](/docs/ui/theme-and-accessibility#keyboard-shortcuts).
